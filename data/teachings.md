## agency

The loop depends on a quiet assumption: that what happens inside is something done *to* a person. Every author here attacks that assumption. Epictetus sorts the world into what is and is not up to us; Machiavelli grants fortune only half of our affairs; Zarathustra turns every "It was" into "Thus would I have it." Agency starts by owning the response, not by controlling the event.

## will

## courage
